What changes when the domain becomes a surface?

On the punctured t-plane there is no globally continuous choice of one root. On the surface u² = t, the coordinate u is single-valued. In this example that surface is simply a copy of the u-plane, projected by squaring. Its origin is smooth even though the map branches there.

Adding infinity gives a sphere mapping two-to-one to another sphere, with branching at 0 and ∞. A branch cut is a chosen way to display one sheet; the root exchange around zero does not depend on that choice.

For a full input loop, θ increases by 2π and the root angle by π. The readout checks u² = t throughout the path.
