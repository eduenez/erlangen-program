Geometry builds numbers

Add and multiply with a ruler.

Take the points of one line as numbers. Joining points and drawing parallels is enough to add and to multiply them — no measuring.

Drag the points a and b along the line, or use the sliders. Gold square: the constructed result.

How does the construction work?

In an axiomatic projective space, these constructions define the arithmetic. Desargues's theorem makes them independent of the helper points and gives the laws of a division ring; Pappus's theorem gives commutativity.
